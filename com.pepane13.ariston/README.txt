Control your Ariston Nuos heat pump water heater from Homey, through the Ariston NET cloud.

The app shows the water temperature, the target temperature, the mode (Green, Comfort, Fast, i-Memory), the boost, whether the heat pump is running and the energy used by the heat pump and by the resistor over the last day.

From the Homey app or your Flows you can switch the water heater on or off, set the temperature, change the mode and switch the boost. The app takes no decision of its own: the hot water logic stays in your Flows.

Pairing uses the e-mail address and password of your Ariston NET account (the same as in the Ariston app).

Unofficial app, not affiliated with Ariston. The Ariston NET cloud API is not publicly documented and may change without notice.
